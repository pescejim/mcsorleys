Last call’s a long way off.

Patrons order Light or Dark — that’s the whole menu. Hold a tap to pour, let go inside the green band for a proper pint, then tap the patron to serve.

Overfill it and it’s foam all over the bar. Keep ’em waiting and they walk.

That’s the shift!

How to pour: Press and hold a tap (or hold L / D) and release when the ale reaches the green band — the inner stripe is a perfect pour, worth bonus points. Short pour? Just hold again to top it up. Wrong ale in the mug, or a foam-over? Hit Dump mug. Serve by tapping the patron (or pressing 1, 2, 3). Tips shrink as patience drains, and the house gets busier — and thirstier — as the shift wears on.
How Tapper ’83 works: Todd, Jim and Martin come through the doors on the left and shuffle down one of four bars toward your taps on the right. Move between bars by tapping a bar, the Bar up / Bar down buttons, or ↑ / ↓ keys — then hit Slide a mug (or Space) and the mug rides the bar to the first patron it meets. A served patron slides back out and tosses the empty mug back down the bar: be standing at that bar to catch it for bonus points. A mug that sails past everybody, an empty that hits the floor, or a patron who reaches your taps costs you one of 5 lives. Last 90 seconds, score big.

House rules: be good or be gone. Light or dark — no third choice, no apologies.
